Solves wordle answers

The "Quick" solver solves for any answer on the orignal 2,300 word answer list of Wordle.
But since New York Times took over Wordle, the word list is not followed exactly and new words get added every now and then, so the "Realistic" solver finds the answer over all the 14,800 acceptable words by Wordle.

Choose "Quick" for testing
